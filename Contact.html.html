Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about contacting us

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call our support number or use the WhatsApp button for immediate assistance.

Do you offer free solar consultations?

Yes! We offer free, no-obligation solar consultations. Our experts will assess your energy needs, provide system recommendations, and explain government subsidy options during the consultation.

What information should I provide for a solar quote?

For an accurate quote, please provide: Your location, average monthly electricity bill, available rooftop area, and any specific energy requirements. Our team will also need to know if you're interested in residential, commercial, or industrial solar solutions.

Can I visit your office without an appointment?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure our solar experts are available to give you their full attention. You can call ahead or book an appointment through our website.

What areas do you serve?

We serve residential, commercial, and industrial clients across North India including Delhi NCR, Haryana, Punjab, Uttar Pradesh, Rajasthan, and Uttarakhand. We're expanding to new regions regularly.
