About Era Solar

Pioneering the solar revolution in India with innovative, sustainable, and affordable energy solutions. We're committed to making solar energy accessible to every home and business.

Our Mission & Vision

Driving India's transition to clean, renewable energy through innovation and commitment

Our Mission

To democratize access to solar energy by providing affordable, reliable, and sustainable energy solutions that reduce electricity costs, carbon footprint, and dependency on conventional power sources across India.

Our Vision

To be India's most trusted solar energy partner, leading the nation's transition to 100% renewable energy by making solar power accessible to every household and business, contributing to a cleaner and greener planet.

Our Journey

Founded in 2015, Era Solar began with a simple yet powerful vision: to harness the abundant solar energy of India and make it accessible to everyone. What started as a small startup with just three passionate engineers has now grown into one of North India's leading solar energy companies.

We've successfully installed over 12,500 solar systems across residential, commercial, and industrial sectors, generating more than 89,500 MW of clean energy and reducing over 52,400 tons of CO₂ emissions.
